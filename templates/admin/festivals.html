Festival Configuration
Jain festival periods pause the six-week study schedule for affected regions — the schedule resumes and shifts forward automatically after the break.
🪔 How this works: When a region's active six-week schedule overlaps with a configured festival, that period becomes a break. No sessions are skipped — they simply resume after the festival ends, and everything after shifts forward by the same number of days.
